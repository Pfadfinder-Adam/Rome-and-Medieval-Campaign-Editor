# Changelog

What each version brings, in short.

## 0.35.5 - 2026-10-28

- About 75 fixes from players' and testers' reports.
- A Linux version beside the Windows one.
- The editor's own license from this version on (versions up to 0.35.4 stay GPL-3.0).

## 0.35.4 - 2026-10-22

- A whole mod, map or faction from zero, step by step.
- Paint right on the 3D map, with Undo.
- Themes and your own look for the editor; a library of climates.
- Many fixes from testers' and players' reports.

## 0.35.3 - 2026-10-22

- Fixes for a campaign that stopped loading and for mixed-up ground colours.
- War dogs and pigs in the unit's battle model, with their 3D view.

## 0.35.2 - 2026-10-22

- Fixes for the map, for names with accents and for mods built on the plain game.
- More things found by Check mod files.

## 0.35.1 - 2026-10-18

- Fixes for game crashes after some changes and for the 3D map.
- Report a bug says in one line why the game stopped.

## 0.35.0 - 2026-10-18

- Bring a whole faction from another mod.
- The campaign map in 3D.
- One emblem for all of a faction's flags, banners and logos.
- Locked factions opened to play; rebels and script places on the map.
- Many fixes from testers' reports.

## 0.34.0 - 2026-10-10

- Battle models move: animations, riders and siege engines in the 3D view.
- Make a unit's card from its 3D model.
- Shape the coast by hand; new land, wastelands and impassable land.
- One Faction editor; Settlements as a page of its own.
- New plugin: Upkeep x 2.
- Many fixes.

## 0.33.0 - 2026-10-08

- Merge regions, and delete many towns at once.
- Drag the map's edges to change its size.
- A religion, a building or a unit from nothing.
- Your own files for a battle model.
- Fixes.

## 0.32.0 - 2026-10-07

- Module builder: control blocks and a block look.

## 0.31.0 - 2026-10-07

- Rename a faction in one step.
- Unit and building cards on hover; sortable tables.
- One write for all windows, with plainer questions.

## 0.30.1 - 2026-10-07

- The town and character windows wait for Apply like the rest.

## 0.30.0 - 2026-10-07

- The Map editor: towns, armies, agents, ports, forts and regions right on the map.
- Map size: add or cut rows and columns; the bigger map (x3) as a beta.
- Module builder: your own plugin from blocks, no code.
- Plugins: Avoid Growth, Sack Settlement, Player Diplomacy.
- Start the game from the editor; Undo after every write; nothing lost when a window closes.
- Mercenaries, the character window and many more checks.
- Many fixes and a tidier window.
